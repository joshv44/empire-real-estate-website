    AREA GUIDE

    Crandon & Forest County

    Crandon is the county seat of Forest County, in the heart of Wisconsin's Northwoods: a four-season region of lakes, rivers and national forest.

    Lakes

    Crandon sits on the shore of Lake Metonga, and the surrounding area is dotted with lakes large and small, from popular recreational lakes to quiet, undeveloped shorelines. Nearby Oneida County adds chains like the Moen Lake Chain near Rhinelander.

    Rivers

    The Peshtigo, Wolf and Pine rivers and their tributaries wind through the region, offering trout water, paddling and private river frontage that is hard to find elsewhere.

    Public Land & Hunting

    Much of Forest County lies within the Chequamegon-Nicolet National Forest, giving landowners nearby access to large tracts of public land for hunting, hiking and exploring.

    Trails & Four Seasons

    ATV/UTV routes and groomed snowmobile trails connect communities across the Northwoods. Summers bring boating and fishing; fall color and hunting season follow; winter brings snow sports and ice fishing.

    Getting Here

    Crandon is on US Highway 8, east of Rhinelander, a manageable drive from Green Bay and the Fox Cities, and within reach of Milwaukee, Madison and Chicago for weekend owners.

    Buying Land? Ask Us About…

    Road access and easements, surveys and boundaries, zoning and building rules, well and septic, power availability, and shoreland regulations on waterfront parcels. We'll help you get answers before you buy.
